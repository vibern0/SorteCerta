# SorteCerta: chosen brand direction

**Decision — 29 September 2026:** Direction 02, **playful and expressive**, is the chosen brand direction. Founder confirmation: “direction 02 completely wins. it's decided”.

This decision selects the playful concept, not the original comparison's second palette (ink and citron). It is the reference for future landing-page and web-app design work.

## Audience and positioning

- Existing USDC holders in Nigeria and Kenya.
- Prize excitement leads the emotional experience.
- Explain saving, eligibility, and money movement in clear everyday language.

## The chosen expression

- Rounded, expressive display typography, using Fredoka in the concept, paired with DM Sans for readable body text.
- Bold color, warm backgrounds, shaped color blocks, playful stickers, and solid offset shadows.
- A consistent identity across marketing and the app, with more expression in invitations and celebrations and quieter treatment for withdrawals and errors.
- Anticipation before checking a result; celebration only after a prize is confirmed.

The [playful cobalt, peach, and citron concept](brand-explorations/2026-09-29/04-playful-cobalt.html) is the saved default reference. Its palette uses cobalt `#2548F4`, peach `#FFB69D`, citron `#E4F66A`, lilac `#D5C5FF`, ink `#17213B`, and warm paper `#FFF9EF`. The founder selected the overall direction; a preference between its three palette variants was not separately recorded. These values are reference colors pending role and contrast checks, not a complete production token system.

## Voice and message reference

**“Your savings. Your chance to win.”**

“Save in USDC. Your balance at the end of each draw determines your chances.”

Use short, warm, direct wording. Keep actions precise: “Check for a prize”, “Withdrawal requested”, and “Claim prize” in their applicable states. Playfulness belongs in the surrounding expression; eligibility, amounts, transaction status, and errors must remain easy to understand. Follow the repository's [product copy rules](../AGENTS.md).

## Scope of the decision

The creative direction is settled. Further work should develop this direction consistently, rather than reopen the original style comparison. Audience testing should assess comprehension, recognition, and usability; founder preference is not evidence of measured market performance.

SorteCerta remains the working name; this decision does not select a replacement name. Final logo artwork, accessible color combinations, typography specifications, and shared implementation tokens still need to be formalized. No production app, PR, or deployment was changed by recording this decision.

The [saved comparisons](brand-explorations/2026-09-29/README.md) preserve the earlier alternatives for reference. The [research brief](BRAND_RESEARCH_2026-09-29.md) retains the evidence and exploration history; this decision supersedes its earlier open-ended art-direction recommendations.
